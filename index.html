How do I get my Instagram Following & Followers data?

Method A: Export Official Data (Highly Recommended & Most Accurate)

  1. Go to Instagram Settings > Your activity > Download your information.
  2. Request a download, select Some of your information, then select Followers and following.
  3. Choose either HTML or JSON format (HTML is usually faster to receive, JSON is also fully supported) and media quality Low (you don't need media).
  4. Once you download the ZIP file and extract it, open the folder.
  5. Drag and drop the files following.html (or following.json) and followers.html (or followers.json) directly into their respective boxes above.

Method B: Copy-Paste from Instagram Web (Quick Check)

  1. Log in to instagram.com on your computer.
  2. Go to your profile page and click Following.
  3. Scroll down to load the entire list, then press Ctrl+A / Cmd+A to select all or highlight the usernames list, and copy (Ctrl+C / Cmd+C).
  4. Paste (Ctrl+V / Cmd+V) into the **Following** box. Our parser will filter out extraneous text.
  5. Repeat the process for your Followers popup and paste it into the **Followers** box.
