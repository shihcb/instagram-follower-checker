instructions

  1. open instagram → settings
  2. click accounts center
  1. go to your information and permissions
  2. click export your information
  3. choose the account to check
  4. select export to device
  1. customize information → followers and following
  2. date range → all time
  3. format → html
  1. in list 3, click import files
  2. choose the .zip instagram sends you, the whole folder, or its following.html and followers_1.html files
  3. the files are read in your browser — nothing is sent to instagram
  1. list 3 shows who doesn’t follow you back
  2. click the star to keep an account in starred
  3. turn on auto-open next profile in settings to open profiles one after another
